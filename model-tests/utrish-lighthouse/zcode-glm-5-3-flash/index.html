About & full prompt

This is an animated Three.js diorama of a real place: the Utrish lighthouse on the Black Sea coast of Russia, at the village of Bolshoy Utrish, ~20 km south-east of Anapa, at the edge of the Utrish nature reserve on the Abrau peninsula. Two lighthouses stand on the rocky island: the 17-metre 1975 lighthouse-monument (arch. Boris Kamaev, memorial to Black Sea sailors of WWII, with its sculpted relief band) and the low 1911 lighthouse, now the little blue-domed Chapel of St Nicholas. A rusty metal footbridge crosses the ~20-metre strait from the sickle spit of the cape; a pebble beach, a small pier with boats and the dry juniper-and-pine ridges of the Abrau peninsula lie across the water.
