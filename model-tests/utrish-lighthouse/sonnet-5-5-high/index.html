About this miniature, the model & the prompt

The place

The Utrish lighthouse stands on Utrish Island at Bolshoy Utrish, about 20 km south-east of Anapa on the Russian Black Sea coast, at the edge of the Utrish nature reserve. Two lighthouses share the island: the 1975 lighthouse-monument to Black Sea sailors of the Second World War, and the small 1911 lighthouse that became the Chapel of St Nicholas.

This is an artistic miniature interpretation, not an exact survey. Distances, the shape of the coast, the strait width and the sea state are simplified and exaggerated for readability. It is a memorial site and is meant to be shown with respect.
