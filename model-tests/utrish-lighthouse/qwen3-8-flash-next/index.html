Utrish Islet · Bolshoy Utrish · near Anapa · Black Sea

About this miniature

What this is

An artistic miniature of the Utrish lighthouse on Utrish Islet, Bolshoy Utrish, about 20 km south-east of Anapa on the Black Sea coast of Russia. It is an interpretive model, not a survey: proportions, heights and the layout are read from photographs, drone views and open map data, then generalised. Positions are approximate; the coastline, the terrace levels and the harbor are simplified.

Everything in the image is generated at run time by code — terrain, textures, water, sky, light. No photographs, scans or 3D models were downloaded or embedded.
