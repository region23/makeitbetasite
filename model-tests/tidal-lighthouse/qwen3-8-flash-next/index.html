The Tidal Lighthouse — animated diorama

A rugged miniature island cut away like a museum display piece: a weathered red-and-white lighthouse on the highest rock, a stone stair winding down to a fishing cottage, a wooden jetty with two moored boats, and an ocean that rolls in as swelling breakers and foam on the exposed coast while the harbour inside stays clear, turquoise and nearly flat. Gulls circle, grasses bend in the wind and dolphins occasionally surface and leap in the open water. Everything is generated in code and rendered in real time; drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.

How it is built

Three.js r166. Every vertex and every texture is produced at load time — heightfield, strata, rocks, plaster, slate, wood, tower bands, netting — with no downloaded images or models. The sea is a Gerstner swell summed from eight components with a fetched wave-shadow and travel-time lag map, screen-space refraction, sky reflection, sun glitter and depth-driven foam; the same wave field is re-evaluated on the CPU so the boats, spray and dolphins ride exactly the water you see.
